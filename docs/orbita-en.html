How Orbita uses your Google data

Connecting Google is optional. You connect it in Orbita: Profile → Google Calendar.

Google Calendar calendar

  • show your schedule and prepare meeting briefs;
  • find free time across your calendars;
  • create, reschedule and cancel events and send invitations — only when you ask.

Google Tasks tasks

  • tasks with a due date that you create in Orbita appear in Google Tasks;
  • tasks you complete in Google Tasks are marked done in Orbita.

Orbita's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising and do not use it to train AI models. You can disconnect Google at any time in Orbita or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; your access tokens are deleted. Details: Privacy Policy.

Your data stays yours

Encrypted in transit

All traffic goes over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Integration tokens are stored on our servers with restricted access.

Servers in Russia

Data is stored on servers in Russia, with backups kept for up to 30 days.

You are in control
